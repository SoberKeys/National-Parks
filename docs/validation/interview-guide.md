# PARTICIPANT INTERVIEW GUIDE

15 interviews · 30 minutes · recorded with consent · $25 gift card.

**Capture verbatim quotes, not summaries. The quotes will change our minds more than the numbers will.**

## Interviewer discipline
- Never defend the product. Never explain what it will become. Never lead.
- Ask "why?" three times.
- Long silences are where the good material lives. Sit in them.
- If they criticise something, thank them and ask for more. That is the point of the call.
- **Cohort classification happens here and it is authoritative** (see `cohort-definitions.md`).
  When genuinely torn, classify *down* — C→B, B→A. Overstating travel intent is the most damaging
  error this pilot could make.

---

## Warm-up
1. Tell me about your last National Park trip. What made you go?
2. Describe your running or hiking — what's a normal week for you?
3. Do you collect anything, track anything, or keep a list of things you want to complete?

## Discovery — and cohort classification
4. Where did you first see this, and what was your very first thought?
5. What made you actually sign up rather than just look?
6. **Walk me through the trip decision. Before you saw this, what were your plans for the next few months?**
7. **Then: what changed, if anything?**
   - Nothing; I was going anyway → **A**
   - I was going to visit, but I changed my dates / route / itinerary / how long I stayed / what I did → **B**
   - I had no trip planned; I scheduled one because of this → **C**
8. *(B)* What specifically did you change, and what did that cost you — time, money, a compromise with
   whoever you travel with?
9. *(C)* **What did you have to move around to make this happen? Would you honestly have gone otherwise?**

## The completion
10. Describe the day, starting from waking up.
11. Was there a moment out there where you thought about the challenge or the achievement?
12. Anything confusing, missing, or annoying? Be specific and be harsh.

## Achievement credibility *(Stage 3 — Amendment 5)*
13. **Does it matter to you that this had to be *earned* rather than just claimed? Why?**
14. If the app had simply taken your word for it — no checking at all — **would the achievement mean
    less to you?**
15. Did it matter that a *human* checked it, or would an automatic check have been equally fine?
    *(This question separates "credibility matters" from "human review matters". The answer decides
    how expensive our verification has to be, so do not skip it or paraphrase it.)*
16. **What did it feel like when it came back verified and the park unlocked? In your own words.**
17. Did you show anyone? Who, and what did you say?

## Payment
18. You saw the Completion Kit at $XX. Walk me through what you thought.
19. **What's the most you'd have paid for it?** And **at what price would you have said no?**
    *(Van Westendorp pair — ask both halves, record both numbers.)*
20. *(Founding Collectors)* $99 up front for something that doesn't fully exist yet. **Why did you do that?**
21. *(Non-purchasers)* **What stopped you?** Be blunt — I need the real reason, not the polite one.

## Second-park behaviour *(Stage 6 — Amendment 6: probe for ACTION, not intention)*
22. Is there a second park you want to do? Which one, and when?
23. **Have you actually done anything about it?** — looked at dates, checked flights or lodging, told
    someone you're going, put it in a calendar, made a booking?
    *(Push for specifics. "I looked at flights" with no detail is a soft action, not a hard one.)*
24. *(If nothing yet)* **What's the actual thing standing in the way** — time, money, someone else's
    schedule, or just that you haven't got to it?
25. If this stopped existing tomorrow, what would you miss?

## Close
26. What would make this dramatically better?
27. Who else would do this? Would you introduce me?

## After every interview — within 30 minutes, while it is fresh

Record in the research journal:

```
Participant:        [first name, cohort A/B/C, park]
cohort_declared:    [what they ticked at signup]
cohort_confirmed:   [your call]
cohort_notes:       [the verbatim reasoning — why this call and not the other one]
Reservation price:  [most they'd pay] / [price they'd refuse]
Second-park action: [none | soft: ... | HARD: ...]
Three verbatim quotes worth keeping:
  1.
  2.
  3.
What surprised me:
What this changes about my view:
```

The last two lines are the ones that matter at the gates. Fill them in every time, even when the
answer is "nothing".
