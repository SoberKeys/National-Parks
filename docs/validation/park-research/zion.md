# ZION NATIONAL PARK — Utah

**Status:** research in progress · **No route published** · **Field verification: not yet done**

## Why this park is in the validation set
The iconic name in the validation set, and the Pa'rus Trail is genuinely well-suited: paved, flat, and
explicitly multi-use. It is also the park with the most serious seasonal hazard profile of the three,
which makes it a good test of whether our safety system actually works.

### ADVENTURE — candidate, not yet selected
A Pa'rus + Watchman Trail combination was the blueprint candidate. **Not researched yet.** The Watchman
Trail is more exposed and involves real climbing; it needs its own difficulty and heat assessment
before it is offered.

## Explicitly excluded
- **Angels Landing** — permit-gated by lottery. Never appears in any route, in any combination, ever.

## ⚠️ September hazards — the highest safety-attention item in the validation set
- **Heat.** Afternoon highs in Zion in early September are frequently in the 90s°F. Any published
  challenge needs a start-before-8am recommendation and a heat advisory.
- **Monsoon season and flash flooding.** The North American monsoon affects southern Utah through
  roughly September. Pa'rus follows the Virgin River. **Confirm current NPS Zion flash-flood messaging
  at T1 and link to the live park conditions page before publishing anything.**

`UNKNOWN` — exact current NPS flash-flood and heat guidance for the pilot window. **This must be
resolved at T1 before Zion is offered to any participant.** If it cannot be resolved confidently,
Zion's enrollment opens later than Acadia's and Shenandoah's. Delaying one park is a smaller cost than
sending someone down a river trail on the wrong afternoon.

## Pilot-window notes (Sep 1 – Oct 15, 2026)
- **October is a materially better month than September for Zion** on both heat and monsoon risk. This
  directly conflicts with the shutdown-driven decision to front-load completions into September.
- `RECOMMENDATION` — if a lapse in appropriations does **not** occur on Oct 1, weight Zion recruitment
  toward the first two weeks of October and weight Acadia and Shenandoah toward September. That
  sequencing costs nothing and reduces the sharpest safety risk in the pilot.
