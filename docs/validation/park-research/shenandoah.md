# SHENANDOAH NATIONAL PARK — Virginia

**Status:** research in progress · **No route published** · **Field verification: not yet done**

## Why this park is in the validation set
Roughly 90 minutes from the Washington DC metro — the single most accessible park in the validation
set for a large population, which directly serves the second-completion question. Hundreds of
trailheads directly off Skyline Drive.

## Candidate challenges

### EXPLORER — Lewis Spring Falls Loop
| Field | Value | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | ~3.3 mi loop (some sources report 2.9 mi for the trail alone) | T2 |
| Elevation gain | ~823 ft | T2 |
| Surface | **"Consistently steep and rocky"** on the descent toward the falls | T2 |
| Access | Behind Big Meadows Lodge / Big Meadows Campground | T2 |
| NPS reference | NPS publishes a Big Meadows and Rose River backcountry PDF for the area | T1 (area, not this route) — [NPS PDF](https://www.nps.gov/shen/planyourvisit/upload/SHEN_BCTrip-Big_Meadows_and_Rose_River_B015-508.pdf) |

⚠️ **Concern to resolve before publication.** 823 ft of gain over 3.3 mi on a steep, rocky, waterfall
descent is **not an entry-level running route**, whatever its distance suggests. Descending rocky
ground at pace is where ankle and knee injuries happen. Either re-tier this as ADVENTURE, or find a
genuinely easier Explorer route in the Big Meadows area. **Do not publish it as the easy option.**

## Hazards to publish (each needs a T1 source)
- [ ] Rocky, technical footing — the primary injury risk here
- [ ] Active black bears
- [ ] Skyline Drive congestion during foliage season
- [ ] Limited cell coverage
- [ ] Afternoon thunderstorms on exposed ridgeline
- [ ] Ticks

## Pilot-window notes (Sep 1 – Oct 15, 2026)
- September is excellent. **October foliage brings heavy Skyline Drive traffic** — a real friction for
  participants trying to reach a trailhead, and another reason to front-load September.
- **Government shutdown risk from Oct 1** — see Contingency C-1 in `PLAN.md`.
