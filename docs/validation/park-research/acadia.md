# ACADIA NATIONAL PARK — Maine

**Status:** research in progress · **No route published** · **Field verification: not yet done**

## Why this park is in the validation set
Best running infrastructure of any National Park: a large carriage-road network plus coastal paths.
Compact, navigable, and within reach of the Boston and New York metros — which matters for the
second-completion question more than scenery does.

## Pilot-window notes (Sep 1 – Oct 15, 2026)
- Generally a strong window: mild temperatures, lower crowds than August.
- **Foliage traffic peaks mid-to-late October**, which is after our high-value completion window and is
  one reason completions are front-loaded into September.
- **Government shutdown risk from Oct 1** — see Contingency C-1 in `PLAN.md`.
